## 6. Benchmarks

**Choice of workload.** In-repo UCR data exists (`data/benchmark/UCRArchive_2018/`, 128
datasets), but the UCR runner (`benchmarks/UCR_dtwc.cpp`) is hard-coded to iterate the ENTIRE
archive (`dataofInterest = UCR_list;`) and resolves data via repo-relative `settings::paths`
— it cannot be pointed at "3 datasets" without editing source (forbidden here). The Google
Benchmark micro-benchmarks (`bench_f32_vs_f64`, `bench_dtw_baseline`) **self-generate**
deterministic random series (fixed seed 42) at representative lengths and give clean,
reproducible wall-times — these are the baseline anchors. UCR data availability is recorded
above; UCR runner was NOT used (would not fabricate a 3-dataset subset from an all-datasets binary).

| Anchor | Baseline Time | Note |
|--------|---------------|------|
| `BM_dtwFull<double>/2000` (full DTW, L=2000, f64) | **31.2 ms** | O(N²) hot kernel |

Caveats for the gate: these micro-benchmarks show run-to-run variance (`bench_dtw_baseline`
reports RMS up to 44% on `BM_dtwBanded`); on a shared 24-core Windows box a single sample can
swing several %. For a robust >2% verdict, re-run ≥3× and compare medians, or use
`--benchmark_repetitions` and compare the `_mean`. Recorded here with
`--benchmark_min_time=0.3s`; use the SAME flag when diffing.
