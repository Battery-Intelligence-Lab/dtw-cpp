# W7d — one orientation helper replaces the copied preambles (2026-10-01)

Branch `pb/W7d` from `143f138` (pb/W7c's tip). Raw data, scripts and the timing harness are in
`2026-10-01-w7d-orient/`; logs in the session scratchpad (`W7d_*.log`). Everything is `[confirmed]` (observed
on this tree) unless marked.

## Band, registered before any measurement

Subject: the fill of a variable-length, banded WDTW Problem (`dtwc_cl -m pam --variant wdtw`, g = 0.05).
data/dummy itself cannot carry it: its lengths 5148–9405 differ by up to 4257, and the fill refuses a band
narrower than a pair's length difference (`band = 200 is narrower than ... The smallest feasible band is
4257`). So the series are 100 windows of data/dummy, lengths 2000–2048 in steps of 2
(`w7d_make_varlen.py`: window w of data_k.csv starts at sample 1000 w, length 2000 + 2 ((4 (k-1) + w) mod 25)),
every pair feasible at band 50 and at band 200.

Measurement: the real binary, pinned to the eight P-cores (`start /affinity C03C03`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=8`),
fill time = the `-v` clock at "FastPAM converged" minus the clock at "Data loaded" (fill plus a k = 3
FastPAM on 100 series). Five repetitions per binary and band, base and head alternating back to back.

Band: for each of band 50 and band 200, median(head) <= median(base) + spread(base), spread = max - min of
the five base repetitions. Wall-clock on a shared machine: the verdict is `[inferred]` under load.

## Results

Gates (base `143f138` and every commit): serial ctest 105 = 102 passed + 3 MAY_SKIP skips
(`test_cuda_correctness`, `test_metal_correctness`, `test_metal_mmap`), the same list and outcomes;
`cpp_conformance` passed (digit-identical); `dtwc_cl -m pam` on data/dummy, 31 runs (`w7d_cli_runs.sh`:
standard, ddtw, wdtw, adtw, softdtw, msm, twe; l1 and squared_euclidean; zero_cost, arow, interpolate;
band -1 and 4300; float64 and float32), every output file, stdout, stderr and exit code byte-identical.

Preambles: 26 orientation copies at base (`const bool swap =` 20: warping.hpp 5, adtw 4, missing 5, wdtw 4,
dtw_dispatch 2; `arow_orient` 3; `soft_dtw`, `msm_distance`, `twe_distance` 1 each), 3 copies of
dtw_kernel_banded's band precheck, 2 metric dispatchers. Head: `core::orient` (7 callers) and `core::run_dtw`
(18 calls).

Step 3 passed its band but leaned slow at band 200 in every round (head slower in 15 of 20 pairs). The
no-LTO assembly the brief prescribes showed identical DP loops; the shipped ThinLTO binary did not
(`lld -lldsavetemps`, post-LTO assembly with names): at base the fat out-of-line `dtwBanded` /
`dtwMissing_banded` / `wdtwBanded` each held the only call of their `dtw_kernel_banded` instance, which was
inlined there with its Cost in registers; after steps 1–3 those wrappers are one-line calls of `run_dtw`,
inlined everywhere, so `dtw_kernel_banded<SpanL1Cost / SquaredL2 / NanAware* / WeightedL1>` stayed out of
line, and on Win64 a by-value Cost wider than 8 bytes arrives by pointer, so their loops reloaded x and y in
every cell. Step 4 copies the Cost into a local in each kernel.

In-process (`harness.cpp`: `resolve_dtw_fn<double>`'s closure compiled from each tree's
`dtw_dispatch.cpp` with the library's flags and ThinLTO, every pair of the 100 series, one thread pinned to
CPU 10, min of the sweeps; distances bit-identical in every build), ratio to base:

| case | step 3 | step 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Standard band 50 / 200 / unbanded | 1.057 / 1.005 / 0.997 | 0.991 / 1.001 / 0.993 |
| WDTW band 50 / 200 / unbanded | 1.068 / 1.021 / 1.008 | 0.998 / 0.993 / 0.987 |
| ADTW 200, ZeroCost 200, AROW 200 | 0.996, 1.017, 0.995 | 0.995, 1.001, 0.996 |
| two channels, band 200: Standard, WDTW, ADTW, AROW, ZeroCost | 0.987, 0.976, 1.035, 1.000, 1.032 | 0.996, 0.986, 1.008, 0.965, 1.027 |

The two-channel ZeroCost closure's DP inner loops are the same instructions at base and step 4, and its
second round alone had step 3 4.4 % faster than base: noise `[inferred]`.

Shipped `dtwc_cl.exe`, DP loops (min-and-add cells) that reload a pointer every iteration: base 63, step 3
about 113, step 4 0 (8 loops of solver and score code reload in all three builds).

Assembly, no LTO (`-S -masm=intel`, the library's flags): steps 1–3 leave every dtw_kernel_linear /
_banded instance and msm_distance, twe_distance, dtw_kernel_lanes instruction-identical (dtwFull's
dtw_kernel_full: same instructions, other register assignment); step 4 removes the Cost reloads from
every kernel's inner loops (dtw_kernel_linear<SpanL1Cost>: 36 reload instructions -> 0) and moves register
allocation; the multivariate band-bounds setup loop gains 4 instructions (O(n) per pair).
