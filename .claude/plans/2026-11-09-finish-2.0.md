## 2. How we diverged

1. **MM grew from one unit into six.** Approved 10-12 as mapped IPC series as a view, the check fused into CLARA,
   compressed IPC read piece by piece, and `cluster()` through `run`. It then absorbed, each on a ruling of its own:
   kernel-scratch pricing (KS, 11-01), a new memory model (RB, 11-02), the end-of-run report with cpuinfo (RR, 11-02/03),
   the `ExternalSolver` seam and the bindings linking `dtwc_cli` (S7a, S8a), a GPU view (S5) and a timing kit of more
   than twenty bands. One unit re-added 251 KB to `dtwc/`, 59 % of what A–G had cut, and 35 % to `tests/`. Nobody asked
   whether MM was still one unit; that was my question to ask.
2. **Rework.** KS: four commits, 53 files, +2,345 lines, approved 11-01 and removed by RB1 on 11-02 after Volkan's "we
   stuck at this RAM limit a lot ... relax it". Byte-exact `--ram-limit` windows were chased through S4, KS and their
   reviews before the soft budget replaced them.
3. **Verification scaled per commit, not per merge.** Each of about twenty steps ran six build trees, pytest, a G6
   assembly diff, mutations and an adversarial review, then a re-check of the review (R1/R2, then R1'/R2'); I rebuilt
   bases a second time to confirm agents' identity gates. The MM work folder holds 35 briefs, 11 plans and 7 review
   folders; about 30 agents ran on MM since 11-01. The discipline caught real defects (a crafted IPC header crashing cl
   and GCC builds, two blockers in the IPC reader's memory pricing, TADPole ending the process on a refused allocation,
   two MATLAB contracts no test pinned), but the later rounds mostly confirmed the earlier ones.
4. **Measurement before shipping.** The merge rule ties MM to a quiet machine, and none has existed since 10-21. Three
   agents prepared a timing kit (planner, prep, follow-up) that has produced no number.
5. **Records as a cost.** PLAN.md had become a log (175 lines, MM's row 70). Handoffs, baselines, memory and DECISIONS are
   each justified and together heavy.

## 5. Process cuts (proposed)

- Gates at a unit's merge: six trees, pytest, the MEX, CUDA. Per commit: one tree, `ctest -j1`, the touched suite.
- One adversarial review per unit, at its merge. I verify by opening the cited lines and running the mutation, not by a
  second base build.
- G6 only for a commit touching `dtw_kernel.hpp`, `dtw_dispatch.cpp`, the fill, swap or assignment loops.
- No planner agent for a plan under a page; no kit-prep agents; a kit has at most six bands; timing is advisory until the tag.
- Sonnet xhigh by default; Opus for core design or kernels only. PLAN at most 100 lines, a handoff at most 40, one
  DECISIONS line per ruling, baselines for measurements only.
